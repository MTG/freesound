Recurrent donations by frequency

FrequencyDonorsDonationsAmount

A donor counts as recurrent when they make at least donations in a row at regular intervals (each within % of the average interval). All the donor's donations are used, not only those in the selected period. A series has stopped when a later donation doesn't fit it, or when the next donation is more than half an interval late. The first donations of a new series are only detected once it has enough donations.
