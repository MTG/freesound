About this study

Study: Validating Sound Categorization in Freesound

You are occasionally shown a short question asking whether a sound has been placed in the correct category according to the Broad Sound Taxonomy. Your answers will help us evaluate sound categorization on Freesound and better understand how users perceive sound categories.

What we record: your response, the sound being evaluated, the time of your response, your suggested category (if applicable), and any optional feedback you provide.

Privacy: Responses are analyzed for research purposes only. No information that could directly identify your Freesound profile will be published in papers, datasets, or other public outputs. We may use aggregated statistics, such as the number of sounds you have uploaded, to analyze trends across groups of users.

Participation: Taking part is entirely voluntary. You can ignore these questions, and selecting "don't ask again" will permanently stop them from appearing.

Institute: Music Technology Group (MTG), Department of Engineering, Universitat Pompeu Fabra (UPF), Barcelona, Spain — the research group behind Freesound.

Contact: If you would like further information about this research, please contact Panagiota Anastasopoulou (panagiota.anastasopoulou@upf.edu) or Frederic Font (frederic.font@upf.edu).
